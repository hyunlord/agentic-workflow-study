# Workspace Policy Refresh

Published: January 15, 2025

The workspace policy refresh has three main goals: reduce operating cost, standardize hybrid work guidance across teams, and improve onboarding consistency for new hires.

Managers must publish team schedules two weeks in advance.
Each team must maintain a single canonical knowledge base page.
The refresh reduces discretionary travel and duplicated onboarding sessions.

Success measures include twenty percent fewer ad-hoc support requests, faster onboarding, and less duplicated documentation.
