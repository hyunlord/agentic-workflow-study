# Operations Rollout Plan

Pilot window: March 10, 2025 to April 4, 2025.
If the pilot meets adoption and support targets, the company will begin organization-wide rollout on May 5, 2025.

Phase 1 trains policy leads and office managers.
Phase 2 launches the scheduling template and escalation form.

The biggest rollout risks are inconsistent local communication and duplicate FAQ updates.
