Leadership FAQ

Q: How is the rollout plan different from the policy refresh?
A: The policy refresh defines the goals and standards, while the rollout plan explains timing, training, and execution steps.

Q: What savings assumption supports the refresh?
A: Finance modeled savings from reduced discretionary travel and fewer duplicated onboarding sessions.

Q: When should tools be used?
A: Use keyword extraction for broad summaries and date calculations for schedule questions.
